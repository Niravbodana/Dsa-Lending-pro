# NeerCred Brand Safety Check

> Internal note only — not legal advice. No trademark registration required for this checklist.

## Conflict check (Jul 2026)

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Exact name "NeerCred" on IP India | Not found |
| Similar fintech names (Neokred, NeuralCred, Nexcred) | Different spelling/branding — low direct conflict |
| Our N icon | Original design — not copied from banks or NBFCs |
| Tagline "Dream Big · Borrow Smart" | Generic phrase — unlikely exclusive claim |
| Partner bank logos on site | Greyscale only — no modification of their marks |

**Conclusion:** Current branding is **original enough** for an LSP marketplace. Risk of complaint is **low** if we:

- Do not impersonate RBI, HDFC, ICICI, or any partner
- Use legal entity name **Neer Loan Solutions** in contracts and footer
- Keep **NeerCred** as display brand only
- Do not use another company's exact logo colours/mark for the N icon
